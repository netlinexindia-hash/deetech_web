Our history
Years of Journey
History
Over the past 4 years, Dee Tech Group Company has evolved from a visionary start-up into a trusted and innovative technology solutions provider. Our journey began with a clear mission: to deliver cutting-edge technology services that empower businesses. In our inaugural year, we commenced our voyage by offering web development services, and creating bespoke websites and web applications for our clients. As we ventured into our second year, we expanded our portfolio to include managed IT services, providing comprehensive IT support to businesses of all sizes. Our dedicated team ensured that networks and IT systems ran smoothly, contributing to our growing reputation. In the third year, network services took center stage, enabling businesses to enhance their network performance and security. We initiated 24/7 IT support to meet the dynamic demands of our clients, offering unwavering assistance.
Year four marked our foray into email services, including business email setup, security, and migration solutions. Our focus on data security and privacy became increasingly prominent as we implemented industry-standard practices to safeguard client information. By the fifth year, we proudly introduced web hosting services, allowing clients to host their websites and data securely and efficiently. Diverse hosting plans were introduced to cater to a wide range of business needs. Throughout our journey, we consistently received positive feedback from satisfied clients, which further fueled our passion for delivering exceptional technology solutions.
In year six, we upheld our unwavering commitment to excellence, ensuring that all our services met or exceeded industry standards. Innovation remained at the forefront of our journey, as we stayed abreast of technological advancements to better serve our clients. A growing team of experts and a loyal customer base contributed to our continued success. As we embark on our seventh year, we are excited to continue serving our clients with dedication and passion. We look forward to exploring new opportunities, expanding our services, and adapting to the ever-evolving tech landscape. MyZeon Tech Company remains steadfast in its mission to help businesses thrive through technology, and we are excited about the path that lies ahead.
